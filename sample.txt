Company Leave Policy
Employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core hours are 11 AM to 4 PM, during which everyone must be reachable.

IT Support
For laptop or software issues, raise a ticket on the IT portal. Password resets are available at the self-service page and take about 5 minutes.
